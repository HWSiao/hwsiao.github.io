Abstract
======
Situated in the critical low-latitude and equatorial anomaly region, the Space Weather Operational Office (SWOO) of the Central Weather Administration (CWA) in Taiwan maintains high-level capabilities for local and regional space weather monitoring. Our comprehensive monitoring framework integrates solar optical activity observations, high-density regional ground-based GNSS networks, and satellite-based radio occultation (RO) data from the FORMOSAT-7/COSMIC-2 constellation. These multi-source datasets are currently utilized to provide real-time space weather status and drive data-assimilation models for global ionospheric forecasting. To further close observational gaps in the "Sun-to-Earth" chain, CWA is actively expanding its infrastructure to include solar radio burst observation stations, aiming to enhance early warning capabilities for radio blackouts and solar eruptive events.  
This presentation will detail our operational milestones and upcoming observation projects. By integrating our regional expertise into global space weather resilience initiatives, CWA/SWOO seeks to establish new partnerships for international data utilization and collaborative monitoring.
