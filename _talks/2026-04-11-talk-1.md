Abstract
======
Similar to Fast Radio Bursts (FRBs), solar radio bursts (SRBs) are transient radio emissions characterized by their high intensity and rapid variability. However, SRBs are physically driven by transient solar activities and magnetic structures, such as active regions, solar flares, and coronal mass ejections (CMEs). Analyzing SRBs provides a powerful remote sensing diagnostic for studying accelerated electron beams and the ambient plasma medium, thereby enhancing the capabilities for space weather monitoring and forecasting.  
This presentation highlights the current status of SRB research in Taiwan. We will present recent SRB observations utilizing BURSTT project data and report on the operational status of the e-CALLISTO Taiwan-NCU station, which contributes to the global solar radio monitoring network across the 45–870 MHz frequency range. Finally, future research directions and collaborative opportunities for Taiwan's scientific and operational teams will be discussed.
