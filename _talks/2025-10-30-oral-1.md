Abstract
======
The FORMOSAT-7/COSMIC-2 (F7/C2) constellation consists of six small satellites that provide high temporal and spatial resolution ionospheric observations at mid- and low-latitudes through the TriG GNSS Radio Occultation System (TGRS).  These dense TGRS observations reduce the data accumulation period required to reconstruct three-dimensional ionospheric structures and to generate global scintillation index maps. Since 2019, the Central Weather Administration (CWA) has been utilizing RO products for operational observation and monitoring, including the most recent 6-hour nearby RO electron density profiles, high-level ionospheric foF2 and hmF2 maps, and global scintillation index (S4max) maps. In addition, RO products are combined with ground-based GNSS Total Electron Content (TEC) measurements to construct the Taiwan Realtime Global (TWRG) TEC Map and the reanalysis product of the Global Ionospheric Specification (GIS), both developed by scientific research teams from NCU and NCKU. Furthermore, RO observations are assimilated into models to improve ionospheric nowcast and forecast capabilities. Since the launch of F7/C2, these products, assimilation systems, and applications have been operated by CWA to provide reliable monitoring and prediction of the ionosphere and thermosphere.   Detailed information on these operational applications will be presented to demonstrate the suitability and value of F7/C2 RO products for ionospheric space weather operations.
